What the app does

EG Battery displays battery information reported by Android and the device, stores local charging/use history, and offers optional monitoring and alerts. Some values are estimates or depend on device support.

Measurement limits and safety

Readings do not certify battery health, charger or cable quality, charging speed, or device safety. Charging input is estimated from battery-side readings and may reflect the phone's own power use and reporting behavior. Do not use the app to diagnose a damaged battery or charger. Follow the phone and charger manufacturers' safety instructions. Stop charging and seek qualified help if a device is damaged, swollen, smoking, or unusually hot.

Alerts and anti-theft feature

Alerts depend on notification permission, Android background execution, device settings, and available battery signals. Delivery may be delayed or prevented, especially after a force-stop or under device-specific power restrictions. The anti-theft alarm is a deterrent only; it does not lock, locate or recover a device, and it cannot guarantee protection. Test and configure it safely. Do not use it where a loud sound or flashing light could cause harm or disturb others.

Your responsibilities

Use the app lawfully and configure monitoring, permissions and alerts according to your needs. Keep your device and PIN secure. You are responsible for decisions made using the app's estimates and readings.
