Privacy Policy

HomeTerms of use

This policy describes the supplied EG Battery Android source build (package com.techs4arab.egybattery). It should be reviewed whenever the app, permissions or dependencies change.

Information the app uses

EG Battery reads battery information exposed by Android and the device, which can include charge level, charging state, temperature, voltage, current, charge counter and capacity-related values. Availability and accuracy vary by phone. The app also records timestamps, charge levels, charging/use sessions and sampled readings to show local history and charts. Charger checks store their time, power-source type, result and sampled battery-side readings. If you configure the anti-theft alarm, a salted hash of the four-digit PIN and its salt are saved in app preferences; the PIN itself is not saved as plain text. Motion signals may be read while the corresponding movement alarm is armed.

How information is used

The app uses these values on your device to display battery status and estimates, build history and charts, and run alarms or the anti-theft feature you enable. Battery health, remaining time and charging input are estimates or Android-reported readings, not laboratory measurements or certification.

Storage, sharing and network access

In the supplied source/build configuration, the app has no Internet permission and no direct backend upload, advertising or analytics integration was identified. The values described above are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to the developer by this app build. Android or Google Play may process diagnostic or account information under their own services and policies; this app does not control that processing.

Retention and deletion

  • History is limited to 300 sessions.
  • Chart samples cover approximately the most recent 48 hours.
  • The 10 most recent charger test results are kept.
  • Preferences, alarm choices and the salted PIN hash remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Turning monitoring off stops future background recording and alerts; it does not delete existing history. You can clear sessions in the History screen. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data. The supplied app disables Android backup and excludes app data from cloud/device transfer rules.

Permissions and background operation

Notifications are used for alarms and the monitoring status notification. A foreground service, boot receiver, vibration and wake lock support user-enabled monitoring and alarms. Denying notifications, force-stopping the app, Android background restrictions, or device-specific power management can delay or prevent alerts. The app does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access in the inspected manifest.

Security

App data is kept in Android private app storage and is excluded from backup in the supplied configuration. The app does not encrypt all local history or preferences with an app-managed encryption key. Protect your device with its screen lock and avoid using anti-theft PIN features as a substitute for device security. No software can guarantee security on a rooted, compromised or physically accessible device.

Children and accounts

The supplied app does not require an account and is not designed to collect personal information from children. It reads device battery signals and stores the app settings and history described above locally.

Changes and contact

If data practices change, this policy and its update date should be revised before releasing the changed app. For privacy questions, contact the developer: mohamed.elsamman.dev@gmail.com.
